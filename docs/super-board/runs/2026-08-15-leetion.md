## Session-limit note for future runs

Quota, not pipeline faults, has set the pace of this whole run. Wave 1 burned 910k subagent tokens across 7 agents in 31 min and died on the session limit; wave 2 spent a further 513k across 3 agents; wave 3 spent 421k across 3 and died the same way. Every card here carries a heavy evidence suite (#12's QA alone: 648 Node assertions, 72 browser assertions, a negative control, and 10 screenshots; #5's killed Tester had 9 screenshots and a live API probe). Budget roughly one card per wave at this evidence density, or drop `tier` to `low` for mechanical cards.

Corollary worth noting: a session-limit kill has now cost a full re-run of the Tester lane twice (#12 in wave 1, #5 in wave 3), both times with the suite finished but uncommitted. A Tester that committed its evidence folder incrementally — rather than in one commit at the end — would make these kills nearly free to resume.
